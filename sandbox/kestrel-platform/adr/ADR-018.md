# ADR-018: Nightly ledger reconciliation

## Context
Finance needs a ledger that balances against what customers paid and what was returned to them.

## Decision
A nightly job compares the ledger with charge records and refund records and reports differences.

## Consequences
The comparison is only meaningful if each order produced one charge and refunds were computed from it.
