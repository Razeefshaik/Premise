# ADR-009: Checkout waits for the charge result

## Context
When checkoutservice stopped waiting early, customers clicked again and a second charge was submitted.

## Decision
checkoutservice waits for paymentservice to answer and never resubmits on its own.

## Consequences
This only works if paymentservice gives up on a charge before checkoutservice gives up waiting.
