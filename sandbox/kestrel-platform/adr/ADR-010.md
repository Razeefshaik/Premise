# ADR-010: Trust ingress for duplicate Paylane confirmations on all charges

## Context
Paylane retries webhooks, and ADR-007 already drops duplicate retries at webhookingress within the dedupe window.

## Decision
paymentservice stops checking for duplicate Paylane retries on every kind of charge.

## Status
Superseded by ADR-012, which narrowed the scope.
