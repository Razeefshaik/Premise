# ADR-003: Cache currency rates in currencyservice

## Context
currencyservice has the highest request rate in the platform and calls the rate provider on every conversion.

## Decision
Cache rates in memory and refresh them on expiry.

## Consequences
Conversions are fast. Freshness depends on the cache expiry and on the provider publishing on time.
