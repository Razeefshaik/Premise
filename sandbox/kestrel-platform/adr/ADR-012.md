# ADR-012: Payment service skips its own idempotency check

## Context
Every charge in paymentservice performed a lookup in its idempotency table, adding one database round trip.
Confirmations reaching paymentservice are already unique, as promised by G_DEDUPE.

## Decision
paymentservice applies each confirmation directly and no longer keeps its own idempotency table.

## Consequences
Charges are faster. Correctness now rests on the uniqueness promise made upstream.
